Hello, this is Lost Ark.

The May 20 regular maintenance has ended. (10:00)

The content updated after the May 20 regular maintenance is detailed below. Please refer to the information so your Lost Ark play is free of inconvenience.

Content Update — Paradise Season 3 Start

Paradise Season 3 has begun.

Paradise Season 3 period: 2026-05-20 (Wed) ~ before the 2026-09-02 (Wed) regular maintenance

※ Paradise Season 3 runs for 15 weeks, one week shorter than the previous season. At the 7/8 (Wed) update — when Heaven Stage 10 opens — an item that restores Heaven challenge attempts will be added.

Paradise Season 3 Changes

Paradise: ‘Heaven’

  • Heaven entry count has been reset to 5.
  • Heaven is provided up to Stage 10.
  • ※ As of the 5/20 (Wed) update, Heaven is open through Stage 3; one additional stage opens at each subsequent regular maintenance.

Paradise: ‘Proof’

  • With the start of Paradise Season 3, the Season 3 Proof ranking begins.
  • Proof is provided up to Stage 15.
  • ※ As of the 5/20 (Wed) update, Proof is open through Stage 3; one additional stage opens at each subsequent regular maintenance.

Paradise: ‘Hell’ & ‘Naraka’

  • An item level 1750 bracket has been added to Hell / Naraka Keys.
  • The types and quantities of the ‘Gem Selection Chest’ obtainable from Hell / Naraka have been partially changed.
  • The ‘Refined Stone of Fate’ and ‘Refined Stone of Chaos (Weapon / Armor)’ obtainable from Hell / Naraka have been merged into a ‘Refined Stone of Chaos / Stone of Fate Selection Chest’. You can pick and obtain one desired reward from the selection chest.
  • A new card pack, the ‘Aspiration Legendary Card Pack’, can now be obtained from Naraka.

Paradise Orb

  • The Season 3 ‘Paradise Orb’ can now be obtained.
  • A total of 2 Season 3 ‘Paradise Orbs’ are provided, obtainable by clearing Proof Stages 3 and 6.
  • ※ Orbs obtained in Paradise Seasons 1 and 2 can still be used as before, but their maximum Paradise Power is fixed — the Orb’s effect can no longer be increased.
  • ※ Orbs obtained in Paradise Seasons 1 and 2 can be sold to an NPC shop.

Paradise Legacy Changes

  • Some base effects and some restoration-stage effects applied to Paradise Legacy have been changed.
  • The 6 Paradise Legacy types (Censer / Whistle / Mask / Chalice / Bell / Mirror) have been changed so that each type has its own unique Resonance effect.
  • The element and trait attributes applied to Paradise Legacy Resonance effects have been removed.

Paradise Legacy Resonance System Change

The way Paradise Legacy Resonance progresses has been changed as follows:

BeforeAfter
Resonance progressed individually per each of the 6 Paradise Legacy typesResonance progressed as one unified track across all 6 Paradise Legacy types
  • In line with the Resonance progression change, the Resonance conditions have also changed: among the 6 Legacies, raising any one Legacy to Restoration Stage 9 makes Resonance available.
  • ※ However, Resonance effects only apply to Paradise Legacies that have reached Restoration Stage 9 or higher.
  • Performing Paradise Legacy Resonance consumes Resonance Stones; the number consumed changes with the ‘Breakthrough stage’.
  • Each Paradise Legacy’s performance rises according to its Resonance rank. Resonance ranks are C / B / A / S / SS.
  • When performing Paradise Legacy Resonance, the ranks of 2 random Legacies (out of the 6) shift according to the probabilities below:

※ The shift occurs regardless of Breakthrough stage.

  • A ‘Breakthrough stage’ now applies to Paradise Legacy Resonance. There are 16 Breakthrough stages in total: Bronze I–III, Silver I–III, Gold I–III, Platinum I–III, Diamond I–III, and the Esther stage.
  • ※ A higher stage’s C-rank effect is equal to or stronger than a lower stage’s SS-rank effect.
  • When Resonance brings 2 of the 6 Legacies to SS rank, the Breakthrough stage advances and all Legacies’ ranks reset to C rank.

Other Paradise-Related Changes

  • With the item level 1750 bracket added to Hell Keys, Legendary / Heroic grade Hell Key IV has been added to the Paradise content shop > Key Exchange tab. These Hell Keys can be exchanged using the Ebony Cube ‘4th Unlock’ entry ticket.

Content Update — ‘Kazeros Raid Act 2 Extreme’ Added

‘Kazeros Raid Act 2 Extreme’ has been added.

  • ‘Kazeros Raid Act 2 Extreme’ is limited-time content running for 4 weeks. It can be challenged during the period below.
  • Challenge period: After 2026-05-20 (Wed) regular maintenance ~ before 2026-06-17 (Wed) regular maintenance
  • ‘Kazeros Raid Act 2 Extreme’ consists of Normal / Hard / Nightmare difficulties.
  • It is entered by pressing the ‘Extreme’ button on the entry UI for Kazeros Raid ‘Act 2: Requiem of the Drifting Nightmare’.
  • To challenge it, you must reach the recommended item level for each difficulty and complete the main quest ‘The Path We Must Walk’.

  • In ‘Kazeros Raid Act 2 Extreme’ Normal and Hard difficulties, an individual revive system applies on death.
  • ※ The individual revive system is identical to the one applied to existing Kazeros Raid Normal / Hard difficulties — it can be used only once per character.
  • ※ The individual revive system does not apply to Nightmare difficulty.

Kazeros Raid Act 2 Extreme Rewards

  • Rewards are granted for defeating ‘Brelshaza, Manifester of Dreams’ in ‘Kazeros Raid Act 2 Extreme’.
  • Defeating ‘Brelshaza, Manifester of Dreams’ grants gold and the token ‘Coin of Fire and Ice’.

  • ※ On the first Nightmare difficulty clear, the Legendary-grade title ‘Lord of the Bitter Cold’ is granted along with the first-clear reward.
  • In ‘Kazeros Raid Act 2 Extreme’, rewards including gold are obtained on clear regardless of whether gold acquisition is designated.
  • ‘Coin of Fire and Ice’ can be used to craft various items through the Kazeros Extreme crafting NPC in major cities.
  • A ‘Bracelet Effect Reconversion Ticket Chest’ has been added to the craftable item list. This item can be crafted once per week per roster.
  • The weekly craft limit for ‘Metallurgy Selection Chest V’ and ‘Tailoring Selection Chest V’ has been increased from 2 → 4 per week per roster.

Content Update — 2026 Avatar Contest Finals Vote

The 2026 Avatar Contest finals vote is underway!

Finals vote period: After 2026-05-20 (Wed) regular maintenance ~ before 2026-05-27 (Wed) regular maintenance
  • The finals vote can be entered via the ‘Avatar Contest’ button on the left side of the in-game minimap.
  • Adventurers with a character of item level 1680 or higher on their account can participate.
  • You may vote for up to 3 entries; duplicate or split voting is allowed.
  • For each gender group (male / female), you may cast up to 2 of your available votes.
  • ※ You may cast up to 2 of your available votes on the same entry.
  • Once voting is complete, it cannot be edited or withdrawn — please choose carefully before voting.
  • The finals vote tally will be disclosed on the following schedule:
    • 2026-05-21 (Thu) 06:00 ~ 05-24 (Sun) 05:59: the tally through the previous day is disclosed daily at 06:00.
    • ※ During 5/24 (Sun) 06:00 ~ 5/27 (Wed) 05:59, the cumulative tally through 5/24 (Sun) 05:59 can be viewed.
  • The single male avatar entry and single female avatar entry that receive the most votes from adventurers are selected as the final winners.
  • The final winners will later be produced as 1 male and 1 female costume avatar each, plus weapon avatars suited to each class.

For full details, please refer to the ‘2026 Avatar Contest Event Finals Vote Notice’.

Improvements & Bug Fixes

Content

  • In Kazeros Raid ‘Final Act: The Last Day’ Hard difficulty Gate 2, when ‘Kazeros, Incarnation of Death’’s HP is reduced to 1 and the phase-transition cutscene plays, characters have been changed to take no damage during the cutscene. The effect is removed once the phase transitions and combat resumes.
  • Fixed an issue in Abyss Dungeon ‘Cathedral of the Horizon’ Gate 1 where, during the pattern in which ‘Archbishop Arsenos’ throws a spinning hammer forward and teleports to attack again, characters who succeeded at Just Guard were intermittently hit by the hammer.
  • Fixed an issue during Shadow Raid ‘Cerca, Witch of Suffering’ where, after dying with no revives remaining, if the revive count recharged at the same moment a cutscene played, the revive UI intermittently failed to appear after the cutscene ended.
  • Applied an additional fix for a recurring issue in Kazeros Raid ‘Act 2: Requiem of the Drifting Nightmare’ Single Mode Gate 1 where, in the mechanic where an ice wall rises and traps the character, the trapped state would, at low probability, fail to be released.
  • Fixed an issue where the item level requirement to enter Paradise: ‘Proof’ was applied based on equipped item level rather than achieved item level.

Combat / Combat Analyzer

  • Fixed an issue where dealing damage-over-time through the skill runes and tripods below did not increase MVP ‘Assister’ contribution:
    • Skill Runes ‘Bleed’, ‘Poison’
    • Reaper ‘Bleeding Poison’, ‘Corrosive Poison’ tripods
  • Fixed an issue where mitigating certain types of damage taken by party members via ‘damage taken reduction’ effects did not increase MVP ‘Protector’ contribution.
  • Fixed an issue where, when Bard used ‘Serenade of Courage’ together with ‘Major Chord’ so that the damage-increase buffs applied simultaneously, the Combat Analyzer’s ‘Supporter Damage Increase Effective Rate I’ figure’s increase rate was irregularly reflected on only one of the skills.
  • Changed Guardian Knight so that, immediately after entering ‘Incarnation state’, the state cannot be released by pressing the Identity key for at least 0.5 seconds, regardless of cooldown.

System

  • When calling the Market / Auction House while using Storage via the Pet function, items can now be registered to the Market / Auction House while the ‘Item Registration’ window is open.
  • On Snowpang Island, if the co-op quest ‘Snowball Fight, Start!’ ends while the character is dead, the character is now auto-revived.
  • When attempting to craft while some materials used in crafting are locked, an appropriate system message is now displayed.
  • Fixed an issue where, when receiving items from the product box with no empty slot in the inventory, the related guidance pop-up did not appear.
  • Fixed an issue where, when the game screen is set to ‘Windowed Mode’, left/right-clicking the ground in the Trixion training room intermittently caused the mouse click not to register.

UI

  • Changed it so the following UIs cannot be called within Guardian Raid zones: Content Guide, Kazeros battlefield board, Core Content UI.
  • When opening the Market from the Avatar Codex UI called via the Pet function, the inventory UI is now also called each time item registration is attempted.
  • Fixed an issue where, when the Market / Auction House UI and the Avatar Codex UI were open at the same time, closing the Avatar Codex UI also closed the Market / Auction House UI.
  • Fixed an issue in the tooltip shown when hovering over the Tortoyk continent Wandering Merchant symbol where the Uncommon-grade ‘Egg of Creation card’ was displayed above higher-grade cards.
  • Fixed an issue where, although a Wandering Merchant had appeared, some Wandering Merchant symbols were, at low probability, not displayed on the world map.
  • Fixed an issue where, if the Guild UI was called during the countdown after pressing the ‘Start’ button in Paradise: ‘Proof’, the countdown UI did not disappear even after the challenge began.
  • Fixed an issue where the Style Book could not be closed with the ESC key while customizing during new character creation.
  • Changed it so the ‘Market Search’ menu is no longer exposed in the context menu of the ‘Romantic Material Kit’ shown in the evolution-material area of the Esther Weapon Evolution UI. This item is bound to the character on acquisition and cannot be traded.

Sound

  • Fixed an issue where, when Summoner used the ‘Surge of Magick’ base skill or applied the ‘Wrecking Train’ tripod, a certain sound effect played repeatedly.
  • Fixed an issue during Kazeros Raid ‘Act 2: Requiem of the Drifting Nightmare’ Gate 1 where, in the ‘Sky Path’ traversal section, a certain sound intermittently played excessively loud.
  • Fixed an issue where a certain skill used by a certain monster in Paradise: ‘Hell’ / ‘Naraka’ played somewhat loud.

Graphics / Avatar

  • Fixed an issue where, when using a Hidden Esther skill during Kazeros Raid ‘Act 1: Trail of Hellfire that Shatters the Earth’ Gate 1 Phase 3, restarting the dungeon intermittently applied the camera viewpoint abnormally.
  • Changed it so that, in some content, when a boss monster enrages, the visual effect played at the start of the enrage does not play repeatedly depending on the monster’s state.
  • Fixed an issue where placing white or gray-toned furnishings in the roster Estate manor caused the placed furnishing’s color to display somewhat darker.
  • Fixed an issue where, when changing avatars while in matchmaking via the Group Support function, the avatar in the Party Lounge was shown as the pre-change avatar.
  • Fixed an issue where the screen briefly flickered when returning to the Battle Preparation room after failing the Kazeros Raid ‘Act 3: Pitch-Black, Stormy Night’ Gate 1 challenge.
  • Fixed an issue where a Martial Artist (male) character’s index finger bent excessively backward when using the ‘Wave’ emote.
  • Fixed an issue where part of the back of the hair clipped through when an Artillerist character wearing the ‘Daily Casual Hair’ avatar performed combat motions.
  • Fixed an issue where a character’s shadow displayed awkwardly when ‘Shurdi’ attacked on certain terrain while playing Paradise: ‘Hell’ as a Summoner.
  • Changed the tendency (virtue) info of the two ‘Non-Tradable’ Wildsoul weapon avatars below to match other Non-Tradable weapon avatars:
    • Sakura Enchanter Scroll — Before: Courage +10, Charisma +5 → After: Charisma +5, Kindness +10
    • Starlight Enchanter Scroll — Before: Courage +10, Charisma +5 → After: Charisma +5, Wisdom +10

Items

  • Removed the two no-longer-used items below from the inventory ‘Material Storage’ > Voyage > Island Items tab: Splendid Feather, Alaqer Egg.
    ※ Each item adventurers previously held was exchanged via Unified Storage for 10 Shillings per item.
  • The ‘Coin of Courage’ usage period will expire at the 2026-05-27 (Wed) regular maintenance. When it expires, your held Coins of Courage and the ‘Proving Grounds Shop’ NPC will be removed — please use them within the period.

PC Café Login Reward / Lucky Box Component Changes

  • Changed the PC Café 180-minute login reward from ‘Legendary ~ Rare Card Pack IV ×1’ → ‘Joyful Legendary ~ Rare Card Pack ×1’.
  • Changed some components of the [PC Café] Lucky Box:
    • ‘Leap Legendary Card Selection Pack II ×1’ → ‘Leap Legendary Card Selection Pack III ×1’
    • ‘Legendary Card Pack IV ×1’ → ‘Joyful Legendary Card Pack ×1’
    • ‘Legendary ~ Heroic Card Pack IV ×1’ → ‘Joyful Legendary ~ Heroic Card Pack ×1’
  • ※ Any ‘[PC Café] Lucky Box’ not used before the 5/20 (Wed) regular maintenance was granted to the product box in item form.
  • ※ ‘[PC Café] Lucky Box’es granted to the product box consist of the pre-5/20 (Wed) component change contents.
  • ※ Granted products can be received before the 2026-06-24 (Wed) regular maintenance and must be used within 14 days of receipt.

Other

  • Fixed typos and awkward expressions in some text.
  • Fixed an issue during the Kurzan North world quest ‘Allied Forces Grand Council’ where, at the ‘Check where Kamain disappeared’ step, Kamain’s final line was printed twice in the chat window.
  • Corrected the incorrectly displayed acquisition condition of the ‘One Who Carries the Will’ title in the Title UI to match the actual condition. This title can be obtained by evolving an Esther Weapon to Stage 2.

2026 Avatar Contest — Finals Vote Event

Finals vote period: 2026-05-20 (Wed) ~ before the 2026-05-27 (Wed) regular maintenance

Event Schedule

PhasePeriod
1 vs 1 Judging5/6 (Wed) ~ before 5/13 (Wed) regular maintenance
Finals VoteAfter 5/20 (Wed) regular maintenance ~ before 5/27 (Wed) regular maintenance
Winning Entry AnnouncementAnnounced separately after judging & voting conclude

Final Winning Entry Production

  • Production target: the male and female entries that receive the most votes from adventurers in the finals vote.
  • Production items: 1 male and 1 female costume avatar each, plus weapon avatars suited to each class.
  • ※ Depending on production conditions and other circumstances, the actually produced avatars may differ somewhat from the original artwork, and the production quantity may change.

How to Participate

Log into the game and click the ‘Avatar Contest’ icon (on the minimap).

Step 1 — 1 vs 1 Judging

  • From 3 of the 1 vs 1 matches, PICK the entry to send to the finals vote.
  • Submit a star rating for each entry, and submit judging & evaluation opinions.
  • TIP: Use ‘View details’ to check the entry’s artwork and sketch stages.
  • TIP: Evaluations can be edited during the 1 vs 1 judging period; when the judging period ends, whatever was last entered is auto-submitted.

Step 2 — Finals Vote

  • The 6 entries that passed 1 vs 1 judging proceed to the finals vote.
  • 3 voting tickets are usable per account: 1 vote for a male avatar, 1 vote for a female avatar, and 1 free-choice vote.
  • The remaining free-choice ticket may be cast as a duplicate or split vote within either gender group.
  • ※ Votes cannot be edited or withdrawn — please choose carefully.

Checking Judged-Entry Details & Tally Status

Adventurer Judging Content & Evaluation Opinions

  • Each entry’s ‘View details’ shows the average star rating and the evaluation opinions that adventurers submitted (e.g. an average rating of ★★★★☆ 4.10 / 5).

Finals Vote Tally Status

  • Use the ‘View tally status’ button to check the in-game Avatar Contest finals-vote tally.
  • Tally viewable: 5/21 (Thu) 06:00 ~ 5/24 (Sun) 05:59 — each morning at 06:00 the cumulative tally through the previous day is disclosed.
  • Rankings and tally status are shown according to the differences in vote counts.

2026 Avatar Contest Finals Candidates — Preview

The 12 finalist entries (6 male, 6 female) as shown in the original notice. Artwork only — no translatable text.

Cautions

1 vs 1 Judging Cautions

  • The entry that receives the most picks from adventurers in 1 vs 1 judging advances to the finals vote.
  • 1 vs 1 judging can be participated in by any adventurer who has a character of item level 1680 or higher on their account.
  • Entry star ratings can be freely chosen from a minimum of 0.5 to a maximum of 5 stars; edits are possible before the 2026-05-13 (Wed) regular maintenance ends.
  • After 1 vs 1 judging ends and before the finals vote begins, you can review your own judging content.
  • Judging content cannot be edited once the finals vote begins — please keep it for reference only.
  • Entries that do not fit the evaluation’s purpose may be excluded from judging in accordance with game operation policy.

Finals Vote Cautions

  • The finals vote can be participated in by any adventurer who has a character of item level 1680 or higher on their account.
  • You may vote for up to 3 entries: 1 male vote, 1 female vote, and 1 free-choice vote, with duplicate or split voting allowed.
    • You may cast up to 2 of your held tickets per gender (male / female) group.
    • You may cast up to 2 of your held tickets on the same entry.
  • Please choose carefully — votes cannot be edited or withdrawn once cast.
  • If voting is participated in through an inappropriate method, those votes are cancelled or invalidated.
  • The average star ratings and evaluation opinions disclosed in the finals vote should be used as reference material only.
    • Disclosed evaluation opinions are drawn from opinions adventurers wrote on the avatars, with some processing applied; some content is published in edited form.
  • The finals-vote tally is disclosed on the following schedule:
    • 2026-05-21 (Thu) 06:00 ~ 05-24 (Sun) 05:59: the tally through the previous day is disclosed daily at 06:00.
    • 2026-05-24 (Sun) 06:00 ~ 05-27 (Wed) 05:59: the tally accumulated through 5/24 (Sun) 05:59 can be viewed.
    • The disclosed vote tally can be viewed until the Avatar Contest finals vote concludes.
  • The male avatar entry and female avatar entry that receive the most votes from adventurers in the finals vote are selected as the final winning entries.
  • The final winning entries will later be produced as 1 male and 1 female costume avatar each, plus weapon avatars suited to each class.
    • ※ Depending on production conditions and other circumstances, the actually produced avatars may differ somewhat from the original artwork, and the production quantity may change.
